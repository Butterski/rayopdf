# RayoPDF 0.5.0 on the harness, 2026-11-24

0.5.0 adds three output options for conventions (`--figure-text=drop`, `--small-caps=keep`,
`--tables=pipe-latex`), a LaTeX mode that reads formulas by their fonts' codes and spells them as
arXiv sources do, table, heading, list, footnote and furniture rules, and fourteen rounds of
output-identical speed work, to the converter of [0.4.0](v0.4.0-2026-11-15.md). Every quality step
landed only through the strict gate (no gated metric of any development or validation suite more
than 0.0001 below its best). Quality and speed were measured on the release's code (`a95ad7f`); the
holdouts (`readoc` and the weasyprint producer) once, on this code. Same machine and harness as
[the baselines](baselines-2026-11-05.md); the release check is
[release-check-2026-11-24](release-check-2026-11-24.md).

## New since 0.4.0

- **Output options:** `--figure-text=drop` leaves out a figure's own text (tick labels, axis
  titles, legends, a diagram's labels) that does not read as prose; captions, subfigure captions
  and sentences near a figure stay. `--small-caps=keep` writes a heading set in small capitals as
  printed, every letter a capital. `--tables=pipe-latex` writes `--tables=pipe`'s tables for a
  reader that takes cells through LaTeX: `%`, `#` and `&` outside math and code as `\%`, `\#`,
  `\&`. Each is off by default; the default output does not change with them.
- **Declared profiles:** opendataloader-bench adds `--small-caps=keep`; olmOCR's two suites add
  `--plain` (olmOCR's normalisation strips only bold and italics), `--small-caps=keep` and
  `--figure-text=drop`; READoc's suites take `--tables=pipe-latex` in place of `--tables=pipe` and
  add `--figure-text=drop` (READoc's ground truth comes from the LaTeX source, which never holds a
  figure's inner text; opendataloader-bench's does). The OmniParseBench adapter keeps formulas as
  written, numbers ordered lists as the page does and reads emphasis as CommonMark does
  ([evaluation.md](../development/evaluation.md)).
- **LaTeX mode:** formulas read by their fonts' codes (mathabx, TeX's math fonts, OAMathSymbols,
  newtx, Symbol), φ/ϕ and ε/ϵ told apart, stacked bars and fractions, radicals over fractions,
  composed relations (`\neq`), extensible arrows, operator names, cmex bracket sizes, limits of
  adjacent operators, x-height overlines, blackboard and script letters, accents seated over their
  glyph; inline formulas take the operand after a trailing operator; a bold letter alone is
  `\mathbf`; numbers around a spaced `±` stay text; check marks are text; and formulas are spelt
  as arXiv sources spell them (primes as `^{\prime}`, `\to`, `\dagger`, a space between a command
  and a digit).
- **Page furniture:** the edge-row model retrained twice (23 features and a guard for sentences,
  then rows up to 1.6 body sizes on the rules' current features); single pages lose scan stamps,
  heads and feet of pages with text only at their edges, running heads over a rule, running feet
  under text that ends high and patents' running heads, while captions at a page's edge stay.
- **Tables:** header rows told by rules, levels, centred labels, cmidrules and bottom-set cells;
  wrapped lines in padded rows kept in their row; columns of figures under a spanning label parted
  by their text; equation arrays and a fraction bar beside a framed formula are no tables; turned
  tables of sentences read.
- **Text and structure:** drop caps start the line beside them; a dash ending a line joins the
  next line's word (`291–302`), compound hyphens stay at line ends, and upright punctuation after
  an italic glyph stays in its word; insets wrapped by two columns are read after their column;
  list items keep their printed markers; footnote references in table cells and notes whose number
  stands alone are found; numbered, centred or italic titles and a division's label over its title
  are headings, a copyright notice or a lead-in inside a numbered list is not; emphasis delimiters
  flank as CommonMark reads them.
- **Speed:** fourteen rounds of output-identical rewrites (eleventh to twenty-fourth), each winning
  back the cost of the rules landed before it, and `src/hot.ld` regenerated
  ([performance.md](../development/performance.md#050-2026-11-24)).

## Tried and not landed

Every quality step went through the strict gate (no gated metric more than 0.0001 below its best
on any development or validation suite); a step that failed took one fallback, then was dropped.
The largest gains refused:

- **More page furniture to the edge-row model:** rows it scores from 0.5 or 0.55 (rows that may
  be headings still from 0.795) would raise olmOCR `headers_footers` by four to six tests
  (0.891 → 0.896) and DocLayNet validation `furniture_f1` by 0.0036, but took one text that
  OmniParseBench's validation split tests for (`text` −0.00035, `headline` −0.00012); an earlier
  furniture rule (`headers_footers` +0.019) failed on the same test.
- **DocLayNet lists:** numbered paragraphs, marker lines joined to their text and bullet glyphs
  as list items would raise DocLayNet validation `list_f1` by 0.064 (furniture +0.010, headings
  +0.004) against READoc validation `inline_eds` −0.0005, a loss that shows on no development
  document.
- **Bold-lead headings:** a bold line over its paragraph as a heading: DocLayNet validation
  `heading_f1` +0.006 against READoc validation GitHub `head_eds` −0.0013.
- **Headings by rank of style:** READoc validation `head_eds` +0.0068 and `head_teds` +0.0065
  against DocLayNet validation `heading_f1` −0.0038 and olmOCR `multi_column` −4 tests.
- **Tables:** rules fitted on one benchmark's pages lost another's: olmOCR's (+10 `table_tests`)
  against DocLayNet validation headings −0.0005; READoc's (development `table_teds` +0.0036) and
  OmniParseBench's (development `tables` +0.0057) against OmniParseBench validation `tables`
  −0.0072 to −0.0076; a filter for false tables over figures dropped real tables.
- **LaTeX mode:** a display reading that raises olmOCR `arxiv_math` by 0.009 moved READoc
  validation `seg_kt` by −0.00014; cmex wide accents and `\Bigg(` delimiters moved READoc
  development `seg_kt` by −0.00013 and −0.00007; two arXiv spellings were left out of 0.5.0's
  (no `\quad` in inline formulas: READoc's second development split `seg_sp` −0.00014; `*` in
  scripts: OmniParseBench validation `tables` −0.00036, its tests reading `\ast` as ∗).
- **Conventions not made options:** READoc counts `<sup>`/`<sub>` tags as text (written as text,
  `text_eds` +0.006 on the development split); olmOCR's ellipsis and dash conventions (+6 tests).
