# RayoPDF 0.0.6 on the harness, 2026-11-06

What changed since [0.0.5](v0.0.5-2026-11-06.md), all in table detection:

- **Ruled tables:** a ruled box holding three or more lines, each with text in the first and
  another column and one mostly numeric column, is split into one row per line; whitespace at
  least an em wide running through every row of a ruled column splits it (a word across the new
  boundary spans both parts); a header row above a box drawn round the body joins the table when
  the box's rules are drawn lines, not shading; leading rows with an empty first cell or a
  full-width span are header rows, and the column-label row under title rows too.
- **Borderless tables:** section-label rows may reach into the first column gap and are left out
  of the gap computation; in the first pass, later rows may narrow a gap to half an em when numbers
  sit on both sides; number-free header rows are left out of the alignment and spacing checks, and
  a header row up to two lines above the body joins when its words stay inside the columns; the
  alignment tolerance is the larger of 2 pt and 0.3 em; five rows of two-column pairs suffice,
  unless the second column holds only page numbers (a contents list).
- **Sideways tables:** detection also runs in any other text direction with at least six lines.

Same machine and harness as [the baselines](baselines-2026-11-05.md). RayoPDF ran as the harness's
`cli` tool: `build/static/rayopdf` (GCC 13.3, `-O2`, fully static, 2,273,456 bytes),
`rayopdf --convert {in} {out}` with its default image output. All numbers are reproduced here.

## Quality

Changed metrics only; odl and synthetic output are byte-identical to 0.0.5. **Bold**: at or above
the best text-layer tool; † also at or above docling.

`table_tests`: 629 of 1,020 tests pass against pymupdf's 627. Of the rest, 111 are on 19 pages
without a text layer (OCR needed); 104 are on pages with text where no table is found (broken text
extraction, figure text missing from the text layer, a table beside a text column, sparse grids,
tables drawn in monospace characters); 117 find no exactly matching cell (cells holding links are
written as Markdown links by design, a label and number run together, sub-columns of booktabs
tables); 62 have a wrong row or column relationship.

READoc arxiv `table_teds` fell 0.011, and has now fallen 0.018 since 0.0.3 (0.489). In the first
half of this round, READoc arxiv aggregates were also used to compare rule variants; that use of
the holdout stopped mid-round, and the final rule was chosen on development data alone and
measured on READoc once.

## Robustness

- Full conversions with ASan + UBSan, images written (2,802 PDFs: synthetic, odl, olmOCR
  born-digital, SafeDocs): 0 crashes, 0 hangs, 0 sanitizer reports.
- `fuzz_layout_tables`, five minutes on the final code: 49,564 runs, clean.
