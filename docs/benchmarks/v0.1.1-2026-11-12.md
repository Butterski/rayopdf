# RayoPDF 0.1.1 on the harness, 2026-11-12

0.1.1 releases the seven steps taken after [0.1.0](v0.1.0-2026-11-12.md) from reading kordoc
([its baselines](baselines-2026-11-12.md#taken-from-kordoc)). Measured on code `2a8a6a8` (core
alone unless the bundle is named); the holdouts (`readoc`, the weasyprint producer) were measured
once, on this code. Same machine and harness as [the baselines](baselines-2026-11-05.md).

Since 0.1.0:

- **Lists of contents** are text, their leader dots one run (odl NID +0.0036, MHS +0.0038).
- **Office runs:** a space a run leaves out is a word break ("furtherdata", "3AI" read apart).
- **Slides:** a kicker over a slide's title heads nothing (odl MHS +0.0061).
- **Heading styles:** a size's paragraphs count in the heading-style share (odl MHS +0.0030,
  READoc-dev arXiv `head_teds` +0.0030).
- **Charts:** turned chart labels are read where they stand (odl NID +0.0008).
- **Papers:** a title block past wide affiliations, flush left or under a split title
  (READoc-dev arXiv `head_teds` +0.0056).
- **Furniture:** a supplement's page number ("S84") goes; a line holding an e-mail or web
  address heads nothing.
