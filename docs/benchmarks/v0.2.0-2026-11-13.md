# RayoPDF 0.2.0 on the harness, 2026-11-13

0.2.0 adds structured output and a library to the converter of [0.1.1](v0.1.1-2026-11-12.md), a
round of table, furniture and heading rules checked on a second READoc development split, tables
read from images by the OCR add-on, and a performance round. The core's quality was measured on
code `56fba35` (core alone unless the bundle is named; the holdouts, `readoc` and the weasyprint
producer, once, on this code); the two code commits after it change no byte the core writes on
any corpus (`d4310cc`, a JBIG2 decoder bound; `536de1f`, which figures go to the add-on). The
bundle was measured on `536de1f` (the READoc holdout's aggregate a second time, after READoc's
development split showed the routing fault that `536de1f` reverts), its scanned suites on
`56fba35`. Same machine and harness as [the baselines](baselines-2026-11-05.md); the release
check is [release-check-2026-11-13](release-check-2026-11-13.md).

## New since 0.1.1

- **Output:** `--format=json` (schema 1: the document's metadata, its pages and their blocks with
  kinds, boxes and Markdown), `--page-markers` (`<!-- page N -->` before each page's blocks) and
  `--front-matter` (YAML: title, author, dates, page count)
  ([roadmap D6, D7](../development/roadmap.md#d6-output-dialect)).
- **Library:** a C++ API (`include/rayopdf/rayopdf.h`, the conversion the command line runs) and
  library archives with `librayopdf.a`, the header and an example; a container image
  (`ghcr.io/butterski/rayopdf`) with the static binary on an empty base.
- **Agents:** an Agent Skill (`skills/rayopdf/SKILL.md`) and an MCP server
  ([rayopdf-mcp](https://github.com/Butterski/rayopdf-mcp)) that downloads a pinned release.
- **Tables** (found on READoc's second development split, `readoc-dev2`,
  [its baselines](baselines-readoc-dev2-2026-11-13.md), and on odl): a label written one line a
  row in a cell spanning rows, numbers-only grids on turned pages (plot ticks) dropped, grids of
  filled boxes with gutters, a table's header row repeated below it, a grid on a chart's axis left
  as the chart's text.
- **Furniture and headings:** a short row in the page's outer tenth set apart by a wide gap, card
  titles over columns of text, a page's lead line, an italic subtitle joining its heading.
- **The OCR add-on** reads a table drawn as an image as a table when its tables fill the image:
  the image's text becomes the page's text, with the rules found in its pixels, and the image is
  not written. Which images go to the add-on is decided as in 0.1.1 (see Tried and not landed).
- **Speed:** CPU dispatch in one place, a dense ToUnicode index, the glyph record 56 → 40 bytes,
  four rewrites of what the program cost, PNG checksums on carry-less multiplies and AVX2, a
  regenerated `hot.ld` ([performance.md](../development/performance.md#ninth-round-2026-11-12)).

## Speed

CPU 3, 0.1.1 and 0.2.0 (code `536de1f`) interleaved, 2026-11-13 10:11-10:19 UTC, load about 1
to 2, nothing else of this project running; the multi-page documents on CPUs 2-5 at 10:21 UTC,
while a fuzz run held CPUs 0 and 1:

| measure | 0.1.1 | 0.2.0 | release bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| odl warm, pages/s (p95 ms/page), median of three runs | 2,214 (0.696) | **2,231** (0.694) | ≥ 2,000 |
| odl fresh process, ms a document (12 rotated rounds) | 0.910 | **0.893** (−1.9 %) | ≤ 1 |
| odl fresh, page faults median | 154 | 147 | – |
| `--batch --workers=1` over odl, task-clock | 96.6 ms | 96.2 ms | – |
| 27 documents of 4 to 39 pages, four cores, default threads | 146.1 ms | 143.7 ms | – |
| a JPEG 2000 page with its image written | 108.3 ms | 108.3 ms | – |

A first warm run while the load was still falling from the version record's conversions gave
2,106 → 2,065 pages/s; the three runs after it, at load 1, agree within 0.1 % each. The program's
own cost over 0.1.0 (its rules for tables, furniture and headings, the OCR image tables, JSON
output and the API: +0.67 % instructions over odl, +2.3 % over the SafeDocs sample) was won back
by the ninth performance round
([performance.md](../development/performance.md#ninth-round-2026-11-12)): a dense ToUnicode
index, the glyph record at 40 bytes (fresh page faults over odl 163 → 156), four rewrites of
what grew most, PNG checksums on carry-less multiplies and AVX2 (synthetic corpus with images
written +3.0 % pages/s) and a regenerated `src/hot.ld` (faults 156 → 150).

Against other tools (`tools/bench.py tools`; the other tools measured on 2026-11-10, kordoc on
2026-11-12, RayoPDF at 10:15 UTC, the method in [v0.0.12](v0.0.12-2026-11-10.md)): odl document
183, one page, 0.97 ms (median of ten rounds interleaved with 0.1.1, also 0.97 ms; pdftotext
16.9 ms, kordoc 773 ms, docling 9,490 ms); SafeDocs 0000348, 33 pages, 11.0 ms on one core (0.1.1
12.0 ms in the same rounds) and 7.5 ms on four (pdftotext 46.2 ms, kordoc 1,667 ms, docling
93 s); peak RSS 7 MiB on one core, 8 MiB on four.

## Tried and not landed

- **A table's rules let its image read as text** (plan step 3.1 and its follow-up, landed as
  `453ce8d` and `4987378`, reverted in `536de1f` before the release): long runs of ink counted as
  paper and wide gaps left out of a row's density sent odl 110 and 122 to the add-on (bundle odl
  TEDS 0.915 → 0.959). With the add-on on READoc's development split, the same rules made the
  frames, axes and grid lines of charts paper and their tick labels text: 19 of 150 papers and
  17 of 150 READMEs sent figures to the add-on that 0.1.1 left alone (noisy alternate text; the
  split's conversions 147 s → 323 s), and image-only pages of app screenshots became pages of
  headings. Counting rules as paper only in a grid of three rules each way kept matplotlib frames
  out but not Excel charts (frame, axis and grid lines). Images go to the add-on as in 0.1.1.
- **A header label across a column gap is one cell over its columns** (plan step 2.3): measured
  against the step before it, every development table metric fell a little (olmOCR
  `table_tests` −0.0029, READoc arXiv `table_teds` −0.0003 to −0.0004 on the development
  splits), and on 12 olmOCR table pages labels set apart (each over its own column) were joined
  into one cell across all the columns; its fallback (a gap of 1.5 word spaces) changed the same
  12 pages. Dropped.
- **JPEG 2000 inverse transforms on AVX2** (plan step 6.6): bit-identical, −1.67 % on the 40
  JPEG 2000 documents against the 5 % the step had to reach (the transforms are about a tenth of
  the decoder's work); recorded in [performance.md](../development/performance.md).
