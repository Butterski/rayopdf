# RayoPDF 0.0.9 on the harness, 2026-11-08

What changed since [0.0.8](v0.0.8-2026-11-07.md):

- **OCR add-on.** A separate program, `rayopdf-ocr`, with its models in `rayopdf-ocr-models/`:
  PP-OCRv6 small (detection and recognition) on ncnn, built static for Linux and Windows; an
  external Tesseract backend for comparison. The core starts it only for pages that need it,
  over a framed pipe protocol ([ocr-protocol.md](../development/ocr-protocol.md)); without it,
  output is byte-identical to the core alone. Engine, runtime and tier were chosen by
  measurement ([ocr-engine.md](../development/ocr-engine.md)).
- **Routing.** Image-only pages, text-bearing images on digital pages, fonts whose text is missing
  or wrong, and text drawn as outlines. Region text on pages with a text layer becomes the image's
  alt text when images are written ([ocr_ml_decision.md](../development/ocr_ml_decision.md#text-in-images-of-digital-pages)).
- **Structure on OCR text,** measured from the page image: size from the x-height, bold from
  stroke width, italic from slant, monospace grids, rulings for the table detector.
- **Glyph-shape recovery.** Glyphs of untrustworthy fonts (TrueType, CFF, Type 1, Type 3) are
  drawn once per distinct shape in the core and read by the add-on, with acceptance gates per
  font.
- **Own decoders for scans:** CCITT G3/G4, JBIG2 and JPEG 2000, identical to poppler (CCITT 1,966
  of 1,966 images, JBIG2 638 of 638) and to OpenJPEG (JPEG 2000, 892 development + 182 scanned
  images) on every development image. Those images are now written as PNG.
- **Exact recovery** of RayoMD PDFs with an embedded `rayomd-source/1` payload, checked against
  the pages; `--no-exact`.
- **Harness:** the `scanned` suite (398 image-only PDFs rendered from the synthetic corpus at 200
  and 300 dpi, five codecs, noise/skew/blur; 36 holdout items), `olmocr-scans` (olmOCR
  `old_scans*`), and two OCR tools: `docling-ocr` (docling with its default OCR, RapidOCR with
  PP-OCRv6 small) and `tesseract` (5.5.0, `eng`, 300 dpi, `--psm 1`); their baselines are in
  [baselines-scanned-2026-11-07.md](baselines-scanned-2026-11-07.md).

Same machine and harness as [the baselines](baselines-2026-11-05.md). RayoPDF ran as the harness's
`cli` tool in two ways: the **core alone** (`build/static/rayopdf`, 3,201,352 bytes) and the
**bundle** (the release archives unpacked into one folder: core plus `rayopdf-ocr`, 7,616,640
bytes, and 31.0 MB of models). All numbers are reproduced here.

## Speed and memory

Harness speed set (200 odl PDFs and 6 multi-page synthetic PDFs, fresh process from Python, one
pinned core):

| run | pages/s | ms/page median | ms/page p95 | peak RSS MiB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| core, `--images=none` | 359.7 | 2.91 | 3.91 | 14.5 |
| core, images written | 144.7 | 3.28 | 27.44 | 96.0 |
| bundle, images written | 9.3 | 3.57 | 126.6 | 565 (the add-on) |

The bundle's median is the core's: 6 of the 200 odl documents have text-bearing images and take
seconds each for region OCR, which sets the total. An OCR page costs about 2.7 s on one core
(300 dpi, small tier; [ocr-engine.md](../development/ocr-engine.md)). In process, release
libraries, CPU 3, odl, images off: 0.0.8 0.665–0.667 ms/page median, 0.0.9 0.667–0.672.

## Robustness

- Core tests pass under ASan + UBSan, ThreadSanitizer, the Windows build (Wine) and the add-on
  configurations (`ocr`, `ocr-windows`: 22 tests each); CLI verifier 65/65; 0 warnings.
- New fuzz targets, five minutes or more each on the final code, clean: `fuzz_image_ccitt`
  (2.25 M runs), `fuzz_image_jbig2` (5 + 30 min), `fuzz_image_jpx` (175 k), `fuzz_common_ocr_protocol`
  (8.28 M), `fuzz_ocr_server` (62 k), `fuzz_pdf_exact` (5.45 M), `fuzz_text_outline` (1.82 M);
  findings fixed with regression inputs (JBIG2 empty-symbol loop, JPX empty tile-component).
- Full conversions with ASan + UBSan, images written, core alone (2,802 PDFs): 0 crashes, 0
  sanitizer reports; 2 SafeDocs files of 250 and 416 scanned pages exceed the sweep's 10 s cap
  (10.0 and 12.3 s in the release build, almost all writing 8-bit PNGs of 1-bit scans; 0.01 s
  with `--images=none`) — a performance item of the next round, not a hang.

## Known gaps

- OCR'd text: inline code, footnotes and superscripts are not recognised; quotes marked only by
  indentation are not found; recognition drops some word spaces.
- Legacy-encoded Indic fonts (about 121 census pages) are neither repaired nor recognised: the
  shipped model has no Devanagari, Kannada or Tamil.
- PP-OCRv6 covers neither Arabic nor Hebrew scans, nor pages turned 180°.
- OCR is slow next to the text path (seconds per page) and needs about 0.6 GB of memory.
- Those of [0.0.7](v0.0.7-2026-11-07.md#known-gaps) still open.
