Sentinel start C08.

> A blockquote paragraph states a claim attributed to someone else. It can span several sentences.
>
> A second paragraph in the same blockquote continues the quotation.

> The outer quote introduces a nested one.
>
> > The nested quote sits one level deeper and carries its own text.
>
> The outer quote resumes after the nested part.

> A quote can contain a list:
>
> - first quoted item
> - second quoted item
> - third quoted item
>
> and a fenced code block:
>
> ```
> quoted_code(1, 2)
> ```

Sentinel end C08.
