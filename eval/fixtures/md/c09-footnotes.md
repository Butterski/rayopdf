Sentinel start C09.

The first claim needs a source.[^1] A second claim cites another work.[^2]

A third paragraph references a long note with two paragraphs.[^long]

- A list item can carry a footnote too.[^list]
- A second item has no note.

The final remark has its own note.[^5]

Sentinel end C09.

[^1]: Smith, J. *Collected Essays*. Example Press, 2020.

[^2]: Doe, A. “On Footnotes.” Journal of Examples 12 (2021): 1–10.

[^long]: The first paragraph of the long footnote explains the context.

    The second paragraph of the same footnote adds a further detail.

[^list]: This footnote is referenced from a list item.

[^5]: The last footnote closes the document.
